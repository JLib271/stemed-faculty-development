STEMEd+ Faculty Development

Open for applications Deadline calendar New listings (RSS) Suggest a program

What is listed: national programs on teaching and learning for college and university faculty and instructors (including lecturers, adjuncts, and two-year college faculty) and for graduate students and postdocs preparing to teach. Each is run by a national society or association, a US federal agency, a national program a federal agency funds, or a national private foundation or nonprofit, and is open to people from many institutions.

Not included: programs a single campus runs for its own people (such as your teaching center's workshops or a campus's own summer institute), state and regional programs (such as MAA section NExT programs), and programs run by companies. Ask your campus teaching and learning center about local programs.

Looking for teaching conferences such as BCCE, USCOTS, SABER, or the ASEE Annual Conference? See STEMEd+ Conferences. For K-12 teachers and informal educators, see STEMEd+ Professional Learning.

    What is listed

    Professional development in teaching and learning for college and university faculty, instructors, graduate students, and postdocs in STEM+ fields: fellowships and cohort programs, workshops and institutes, online courses, webinar series, mentoring networks, coaching, certificate programs, and workshops brought to your department. Each program is run by a national scholarly or professional society or association, a US federal agency, a national program funded by a US federal agency, or a national private foundation or nonprofit, and is open to people from many institutions. If we are not sure a program meets that rule, it is not listed. Each file is checked against the provider's own pages on the date shown.

    Programs a single campus runs for its own people are not included, and neither are state or regional programs (such as MAA section NExT programs or a university's regional summer institute) or programs run by companies. Teaching conferences are on STEMEd+ Conferences.

    Disciplines use the same categories as STEMEd+ Jobs, Funding, Conferences, the STEMEd+ Digest, and the Graduate Programs list; computer science is under Engineering and statistics under Mathematics. Programs open to all STEM+ fields appear under every discipline, and programs open to any career stage appear under every stage.
